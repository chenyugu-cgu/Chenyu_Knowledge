# Discrete Mathematics

Discrete mathematics studies countable, distinct structures — integers, graphs, logical statements, finite sets — as opposed to the continuous objects of calculus. It is the mathematical foundation of computer science, digital systems, and algorithms.

## Why It Matters Here

- **Logic and proofs** underpin correct reasoning and program verification.
- **Combinatorics** counts possibilities (probability, complexity, coding).
- **Graph theory** models networks, circuits, and robot [motion planning](../../app/robotics/planning.md).
- **Recurrences** describe the cost of recursive [algorithms](../../cs/algorithms/README.md).
