# Chemistry

Chemistry studies matter — its composition, structure, properties, and transformations. For engineers it underpins materials, energy, corrosion, batteries, and especially the molecular machinery of biology essential to biomedical engineering.

## Why It's Here

- **Materials** — bonding determines mechanical and electrical properties. See [Crystal Structures](../../app/materials-science/crystals.md).
- **Energy** — combustion, batteries, fuel cells are chemical. See [Power Cycles](../../eng/thermo/cycles.md).
- **Biomedical** — biochemistry is the language of cells, drugs, and biomaterials. See [Biomaterials](../../app/biomed/biomaterials.md), [Drug Delivery](../../app/biomed/drug-delivery.md).
- **Corrosion and electrochemistry** govern device lifetime and sensors.
