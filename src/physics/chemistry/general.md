# General Chemistry

The foundations: atomic structure, the periodic table, bonding, and how reactions are quantified.

## Atomic Structure and the Periodic Table

Atoms have a nucleus (protons + neutrons) surrounded by electrons in quantized orbitals (see [Quantum Mechanics](../modern/quantum.md)). The **periodic table** organizes elements by atomic number; periodic trends — atomic radius, ionization energy, electronegativity — follow electron configuration and predict reactivity.

## Chemical Bonding

| Bond | Mechanism | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ionic | electron transfer; electrostatic attraction | NaCl |
| Covalent | shared electron pairs | H₂O, diamond |
| Metallic | delocalized electron "sea" | Cu, Fe |
| Intermolecular | van der Waals, hydrogen bonds | water cohesion, DNA |

Bond type sets a material's stiffness, melting point, and conductivity — the link to [materials science](../../app/materials-science/README.md).

## Stoichiometry

Chemistry is quantitative. The **mole** (\\(N_A = 6.022\times10^{23}\\)) connects atomic and macroscopic scales. Balanced equations conserve mass and atoms:
\\[
2\,\text{H}_2 + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\,\text{H}_2\text{O}.
\\]
Stoichiometry computes yields, limiting reagents, and concentrations (molarity \\(M = \text{mol}/\text{L}\\)).

## Reaction Types

Acid–base (proton transfer, measured by pH \\(= -\log[\text{H}^+]\\)), redox (electron transfer), precipitation, and combustion. The pH of body fluids and buffer systems is central to physiology and [bioinstrumentation](../../app/biomed/instrumentation.md).

## States of Matter

Gas laws (\\(pV = nRT\\)), phase changes, and solutions connect to [thermodynamics](../../eng/thermo/substances.md). Colligative properties (osmotic pressure) matter for cells and [drug delivery](../../app/biomed/drug-delivery.md).
