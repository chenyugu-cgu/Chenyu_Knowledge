# Modern Physics

Modern physics covers the 20th-century revolutions — quantum mechanics and statistical mechanics — and their consequences for the behavior of matter. These ideas underpin semiconductors, lasers, MRI, materials science, and nanotechnology.

## Why Engineers Need It

- **Semiconductors and electronics** rest on quantum band theory. See [Semiconductors](../../eng/electrical/semiconductors.md), [Solid-State Physics](solid-state.md).
- **Statistical mechanics** explains thermodynamics from the atom up and grounds [entropy](../../eng/thermo/entropy.md).
- **MRI, lasers, and spectroscopy** are quantum phenomena exploited in [medical imaging](../../app/biomed/imaging.md).
- **Materials** properties emerge from quantum structure. See [Crystal Structures](../../app/materials-science/crystals.md).

- [Statistical Mechanics](statistical-mechanics.md) — from microstates to thermodynamics.
