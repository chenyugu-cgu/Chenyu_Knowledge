# Waves and Optics

Waves carry energy and information without transporting matter. This section covers the wave phenomena common to acoustics, optics, and electromagnetics — and the optics that underpins [medical imaging](../../app/biomed/imaging.md), lasers, and sensors.

## The Unifying Equation

Almost every wave obeys the **wave equation**
\\[
\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2\nabla^2 u,
\\]
whether \\(u\\) is air pressure (sound), a field component (light), or string displacement. The same mathematics recurs across domains.

## Two Regimes of Optics

- **Geometric optics** — when wavelengths are tiny compared to objects, light travels in rays; lenses and mirrors form images. See [Geometric Optics](geometric-optics.md).
- **Wave optics** — when feature sizes approach the wavelength, interference and diffraction dominate. See [Wave Optics](wave-optics.md).
