# Thermodynamics

Thermodynamics is the science of **energy, heat, work, and their transformations**. It sets the ultimate limits on engines, refrigerators, power plants, and chemical processes, telling us not only how much energy a process needs but in which direction it can spontaneously go.

## Core Concepts

- **System and surroundings:** the region under study and everything else, separated by a boundary. Systems are **closed** (no mass crosses), **open** (control volume, mass flows), or **isolated** (nothing crosses).
- **Properties:** measurable quantities — pressure \\(p\\), temperature \\(T\\), volume \\(V\\), internal energy \\(U\\), enthalpy \\(H\\), entropy \\(S\\). **Intensive** properties (\\(p, T\\)) are independent of size; **extensive** (\\(V, U\\)) scale with it.
- **State and process:** the condition of a system is its state; a path between states is a process (isothermal, adiabatic, isobaric, isochoric).

## The Four Laws

| Law | Statement | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Zeroth | thermal equilibrium is transitive | temperature is well-defined |
| First | energy is conserved | \\(\Delta U = Q - W\\) |
| Second | entropy of an isolated system never decreases | direction & efficiency limits |
| Third | entropy → 0 as \\(T\to 0\\) | absolute entropy reference |
