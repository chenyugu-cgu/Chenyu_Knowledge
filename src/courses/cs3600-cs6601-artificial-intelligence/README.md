# Artificial Intelligence

This section covers the core of artificial intelligence as a set of problem types. It begins with game playing and search, moves to constraint satisfaction, and then to reasoning under uncertainty with probability, Bayesian networks, filtering and hidden Markov models. It continues with logic and planning, decision making with Markov decision processes and reinforcement learning, and neural networks. It ends with bias and the societal questions the methods raise.

The chapters draw on two introductory courses in artificial intelligence (CS 6601 and CS 3600). Where the two courses cover the same topic, such as search, probability and filtering, each topic is written once. The one-page Machine Learning overview, the [Reinforcement Learning](../cs7642-reinforcement-learning/README.md) and [Deep Learning](../cs7643-deep-learning/README.md) subsections continue the methods where they are used.
