# Bibliography

A selection of standard texts and references behind the material in this book, grouped by area. These are the works the author has found most useful for review and for going deeper than a cookbook allows.

## Mathematics

- Stewart, J. *Calculus: Early Transcendentals.* Cengage.
- Strang, G. *Introduction to Linear Algebra.* Wellesley–Cambridge Press.
- Boyd, S. & Vandenberghe, L. *Convex Optimization.* Cambridge University Press. (free online)
- Ross, S. *A First Course in Probability.* Pearson.
- Burden, R. & Faires, J. *Numerical Analysis.* Cengage.

## Signals, Systems, and Control

- Oppenheim, A., Willsky, A. & Nawab, S. *Signals and Systems.* Pearson.
- Oppenheim, A. & Schafer, R. *Discrete-Time Signal Processing.* Pearson.
- Ogata, K. *Modern Control Engineering.* Pearson.
- Franklin, G., Powell, J. & Emami-Naeini, A. *Feedback Control of Dynamic Systems.* Pearson.
- Åström, K. & Murray, R. *Feedback Systems: An Introduction for Scientists and Engineers.* Princeton. (free online)

## Engineering Sciences

- Hibbeler, R. *Engineering Mechanics: Statics & Dynamics.* Pearson.
- Beer, F. et al. *Mechanics of Materials.* McGraw-Hill.
- Çengel, Y. & Boles, M. *Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach.* McGraw-Hill.
- White, F. *Fluid Mechanics.* McGraw-Hill.
- Sedra, A. & Smith, K. *Microelectronic Circuits.* Oxford University Press.

## Computation, ML, and Robotics

- Bishop, C. *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning.* Springer.
- Hastie, T., Tibshirani, R. & Friedman, J. *The Elements of Statistical Learning.* Springer. (free online)
- Goodfellow, I., Bengio, Y. & Courville, A. *Deep Learning.* MIT Press. (free online)
- Sutton, R. & Barto, A. *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction.* MIT Press. (free online)
- Siciliano, B. et al. *Robotics: Modelling, Planning and Control.* Springer.
- Lynch, K. & Park, F. *Modern Robotics.* Cambridge University Press. (free online)

## Tools and Documentation

- [mdBook documentation](https://rust-lang.github.io/mdBook/) — the engine that builds this book.
- NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, and PyTorch official documentation.
